SOCIALFLOW LOCATION SUGGESTIONS PATCH

1. Stop SocialFlow (Ctrl+C).
2. Extract this ZIP over your current Final5 project folder.
3. Choose Replace files in destination.
4. Open your current .env and add:

   GEOAPIFY_API_KEY="YOUR_GEOAPIFY_KEY"

   Optional for India-only results:
   LOCATION_SEARCH_COUNTRY_CODE="in"

   Optional Pune bias:
   LOCATION_SEARCH_BIAS="proximity:73.8567,18.5204"

5. Start again with RUN-SOCIALFLOW.bat.
6. Composer > Instagram finishing tools > Location.
7. Type 2+ characters. Suggestions appear automatically.

The patch does NOT overwrite your .env or db/custom.db.
